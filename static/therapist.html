Motion
Movement

Smooth slows down at each side; Steady keeps one speed; Jump snaps from side to side (quick "saccade" eye movements, based on Christman et al., 2003).

The pause is part of each sweep, so it doesn't change the speed. It can take up to half of each sweep.

Sound

Plays a tone in the left ear, then the right, and shows "Left" / "Right" on that side of the client screen, so the client can check the headphones are on the right way round.

Left / right balance

For a client who hears less on one side: move it toward the weaker ear to turn the other ear down.

Leave the delay at 0 for wired headphones. Bluetooth headphones often need 150–250 ms so the sound lines up with the light. Tap the number to type a value, such as 0.

Dual attention
How often

Gives the client something to watch for while following the light: count the colour flashes, or say each number or letter aloud. The answers show under the counter. Changes apply from the next set.

Colour flashes may not be suitable for people with photosensitive epilepsy or sensitivity to flashing light.

Presets

A saved preset includes everything, even the sound delay and balance, so you can keep one per client. The arrows change the order; on a computer, the first ten load with keys 1–9 and 0.
