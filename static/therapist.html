Sound
Left / right balance

For a client who hears less on one side: move it toward the weaker ear to turn the other ear down.

Leave the delay at 0 for wired headphones. Bluetooth headphones often need 150–250 ms so the sound lines up with the light.

Dual attention
How often

Gives the client something to watch for while following the light: count the colour flashes, or say each number or letter aloud. The answers show under the counter. Changes apply from the next set.

Colour flashes may not be suitable for people with photosensitive epilepsy or sensitivity to flashing light.

Presets

A saved preset includes everything, even the sound delay and balance, so you can keep one per client. The first three are the buttons under Start. The arrows change the order; on a computer, the first ten load with keys 1–9 and 0.
