Phone can't connect?

  1. iPhone with Chrome, Firefox, Edge or BraveSettings → Privacy & Security → Local Network: turn it on for that browser. Safari doesn't need this.
  2. Same Wi-FiThis phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the computer. Turn off mobile data to be sure.
  3. VPN or ad blockerTurn off any VPN or ad-blocking app on this phone.
  4. FirewallAllow EyePath through the computer's firewall.
  5. Clinic or guest Wi-FiThese often block devices from reaching each other. Turn on this phone's hotspot and join the computer to it.
